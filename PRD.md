Project Title: Babcock Student Marketplace

1. Product Overview

Question: What is the product?

Our product is an online marketplace created specifically for Babcock University students. It allows students to buy and sell items such as textbooks, clothes, electronics, shoes, and other things they may need while in school. The website will make it easier for students to find products they are interested in and connect with other students who want to buy or sell them. It will provide a simple and convenient way for students to trade items within the university community.

2. Problem Statement

Question: What Problem are we solving

Students sometimes find it difficult to buy or sell items within the school. Some students have belongings they no longer need but do not know where to sell them, while others are looking for affordable products but cannot easily find them. Our marketplace will solve this problem by bringing buyers and sellers together on one platform where they can easily find, advertise, and purchase items.  

3. Business Objectives

Question: What does the business want

Our main objective is to make buying and selling easier for Babcock University students. We want to create a platform where students can find affordable items without having to search in different places. The marketplace will also help students make better use of their belongings by allowing them to sell items they no longer need. In the long run, we hope to build a trusted marketplace that serves the needs of the university community.

4. Target Users

Question: Who will use the product

Our main target users are Babcock University students who want to buy or sell items within the school. This includes students looking for textbooks, clothes, electronics, shoes, and other useful belongings. The platform will also be useful for students who want to sell their used items at affordable prices or find products from other students without much difficulty.

5. Stakeholders

Question: Who is involved or affected?

The main stakeholders are Babcock University students, who will use the platform to buy and sell items. Our development team will be responsible for building and maintaining the website. Marketplace administrators will help manage the platform, monitor activities, and ensure that users follow the marketplace rules. The university community may also benefit from the platform because it encourages students to exchange useful items and makes products more accessible.

6. User Stories

Question: What do users want to accomplish

As a student, i want to find products easily so i can buy what i need. As a seller, i also want to post items so other students who are interested can buy them

As a student, I want to create an account using my Babcock University student email so that I can access the marketplace.

As a buyer, I want to search for products and view their details so that I can find items that meet my needs.

As a seller, I want to post items with pictures, descriptions, and prices so that other students can see and purchase them.

As a buyer, I want to contact sellers so that I can ask questions and arrange how to purchase an item.

As a seller, I want to edit or remove my listings so that I can update information or remove items that are no longer available.

7. Functional Requirements

Question: What must the system do?

The website should allow students to register using their Babcock University student email address and log in securely to their accounts. Users should be able to search for products, view available items, and see important details such as pictures, descriptions, prices, and product conditions. Sellers should be able to upload and manage their product listings. The website should also provide a way for interested buyers to contact sellers and make enquiries about products.

8. Non-FUnctional Requirements

Question: How well must it operate?

The website should be easy to use and have a simple design that students can understand without difficulty. It should work properly on mobile phones, tablets, and computers. The platform should load quickly and provide a smooth experience for users. It must also protect users' personal information and ensure that their accounts are kept secure.

9. Scope and Priorities

Question: What is included in the MVP?

For the first version of the marketplace, we will focus on the most important features needed to make the website useful. These include student registration, login, product listings, product details, and the ability to search for items. We will also include basic features that allow sellers to upload and manage their products. More advanced features, such as online payments and delivery services, may be added in future versions.

10. Acceptance Criteria

Question: How do we verify features?

The website should allow students to create accounts using valid student email addresses and log in successfully. Users should be able to view available products, search for items, and see their details. Sellers should be able to post products with the required information and successfully manage their listings. The website should also work properly on different devices and provide a simple and reliable experience for users.

11. Assumptions and Open questions

Question: What remains unconfirmed?

We assume that students have access to the internet and can use their Babcock University student email addresses for registration. We also assume that students will be willing to buy and sell items through the platform. Some questions still need to be answered, such as how payments will be made, how buyers and sellers will arrange product delivery or collection, and how the platform will handle fake listings or dishonest users. These details will be considered as the project develops.
