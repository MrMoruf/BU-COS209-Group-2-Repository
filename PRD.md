1. Product Overview

Question: What is the product?

Our product is an online market place created for Babcock University Students. It allows students to buy and sell items such as textbooks, clothes, electronics, and things which they might need. This website would make it easier for students to find the products they need and connect with other students who would like to buy or sell an item

2. Problem Statement

Question: What Problem are we solving

Students sometimes find it hard to buy or sell items within the school. Our market place will help them find and sell items in one place

3. Business Objectives

Question: What does the business want

We want to make buying and selling easier for Babcock Students and help them find affordable items without stress

4. Target Users

Question: Who will use the product

Our main users are Babcock students who want to buy or sell items like clothes, textbooks, electronics and other belongings

5. Stakeholders

Question: Who is involved or affected?

The main stakeholders are students, our development team, marketplace administrators and the university community

6. User Stories

Question: What do users want to accomplish

As a student, i want to find products easily so i can buy what i need. As a seller, i also want to post items so other students who are interested can buy them

7. Functional Requirements

Question: What must the system do?

The website should allow students register with their student email (e.g globalster1212@student.babcock.edu.ng), login, search for products, view items, and post things they want to sell

8. Non-FUnctional Requirements

Question: How well must it operate?

The website should be easy to use, work well on mobile phones, tablets or computers and must keep the users information safe.

9. Scope and Priorities

Question: What is included in the MVP?

For the first version, we are focusing on registration, login, product listings and the ability to search for items

10. Acceptance Criteria

Question: How do we verify features?

Users should be able to create accounts, log in, view products and post items successfully

11. Assumptions and Open questions

Question: What remains unconfirmed?

We assume students have internet access. and since they are students of the school, the student email will be required for their registration and also decide how payments would be made
